📖 Cultural Heritage

Buddhism Arrived in
Sikkim in the 7th Century

Guru Padmasambhava — known as Guru Rinpoche — is believed to have visited Sikkim, consecrating the land as a "Hidden Land" (Beyul). The monasteries built across the four valleys are repositories of Himalayan Buddhist art, manuscripts, and oral traditions spanning over 1,400 years.
